# Research master plan

Evidence labels: **CONFIRMED** = primary source or direct, recorded observation; **INFERRED** = explicitly reasoned from evidence; **HYPOTHESIS** = testable proposal; **UNKNOWN** = not established. Official marketing is evidence of a claim, not independent proof of effectiveness. Date all changing information.

| Order | Research question | Method / output | Completion criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is publicly observable about Seer and onboarding? | Public product and documentation, browser snapshots; reports 02–06 | Direct observation separated from documented behavior; inaccessible authenticated flows marked unknown. |
| 2 | How do comparable products and prices differ? | Official product and pricing pages, report 12 | Each supported feature linked; missing feature is unknown unless verified. |
| 3 | What are reusable open-source patterns? | Scoped source and license inspection, graph where feasible, reports 08–10 | Boundaries and provenance established; graph extraction limits stated. |
| 4 | How to validate and secure a prototype? | Primary security and benchmark research, reports 11, 13–15 | Testable metrics, abstention and cost assumptions. |
| 5 | What to build later? | Synthesis, reports 16–21 | Priorities, dependency gates, risks and remaining questions explicit. |

No application code, accounts, purchases or production changes are authorized by this phase.
